# Routines: first-class n-track choreography, separate from Transition

(Named "Move" for a few hours on 2026-08-24; renamed Routine the same day — "Sequence" was vetoed as hopelessly overloaded.)

Real-session analysis of the relentless groove playlist (2026-08-24, sessions 4–49) showed recurring multi-deck weaves — e.g. Under the Waves→Sovereign→Call On Me and Bangarang→Full Send→Tech Cvlt — that decompose into entries + Cameo pins + overlapping windows only awkwardly, and whose linearization is genuinely ambiguous (nested guests admit both entry and ornament readings). We chose to encode these losslessly as **Routines**: a recorded n-track (n ≥ 3) passage with an enters-with/exits-with boundary contract, window anchored on the first track's timeline, interior on a relative clock, automation addressing entry-ordered **cast slots** that the Conductor maps onto free decks at play time.

Decisions bundled here:

- **Separate artifact, deliberately convergent.** Transition keeps its identity (it is the load-bearing center of the model — library, discovery, pins, editor, templates); Move anatomy is defined as the n-wide generalization so later unification is mechanical. **n=2 Routines are forbidden** — a 2-cast routine is a Transition — so the same choreography can never exist under two representations.
- **Suggestion-first provenance.** Session event logs already capture everything (ADR 0033); a miner marks candidate spans on the Session timeline, a human confirms one into a deck-literal **Routine Take**, promotion re-addresses deck→slot and rebases the interior clock to beats (beat-domain, so a Routine replays under any Set tempo; gesture-level lane vectorization waits for the Routine editor). No liberal auto-minting: analysis showed 128 of 144 detected weave returns were **Practice reps** (backward seeks in the away-gap, or pair-isolated fader-drill alternation), so unattended capture would flood history with rehearsal garbage. Exception: an unconfirmed candidate whose cast matches a Set's next-n entries may be offered in the pin picker at lowest trust — the sequence match evidences intent.
- **Set integration.** A Routine pins onto the adjacency leaving its first track and covers the following adjacencies; offerable exactly when its cast equals the next n Set entries and it ends the covered sequence. Cameo pins remain the vocabulary for one-off, non-contiguous ornaments (measured: Routines absorb all recurring choreography in relentless groove; 7 one-off ornaments remain cameo-shaped).
- **Deferred:** the Routine editor (generalize the kind-aware Transition editor to slot-aware later; v1 review is replay + boundary trim), and Routine evidence radiating into discovery tiers.

Alternatives rejected: pure decomposition (entries + cameos + overlapping-window lane authority) — expressible but paperwork-heavy for recurring weaves and blind to their unit-nature; unifying Transition into Move now — a big-bang rename of the model's center for zero new capability.

Amendments (same day): a Routine's natural boundary is a solo moment (concurrency ≤1 — the miner splits candidate spans there; e.g. the s19 finale is two chained Routines, #17–20 and #20–23, breaking at Bangarang's solo); two Routines may chain through a shared boundary track, covering disjoint adjacencies; v1 playback is mechanical slot-remapped, beat-rebased event replay of the raw recording — idealized lanes come later with the editor.
