Shared vocabulary 7 terms
Beat 0
Bring the opening in earlier; keep the future drop aligned.
Workshop section / half
64 / 32 beats. In the app, Section instead means an analysis-labeled span.
Hyperbar
A power-of-two beat unit greater than4.
Double drop
A later incoming drop over an ongoing track, not an accidental replay of the same drop.
Decisive trade
The quick mids/bass exchange wanted for this NR/Fireflies handoff, not every mix.
Musical pickup / anacrusis
The short lead-in before Another Planet's full drums; exact length tentative. App Pickup instead means returning control to Conductor.
Clean
Intentional drops, phrase alignment and the right vocal/bass roles. Outgoing jumps and scratches may belong.
What's still open Fidelity + method

Recorded filter moves survived, but their historical voicing did not. Aim for steady perceived level without booming or clipping. Vocal detection cannot reliably judge a mix. Flow is a clue; repeated practice is not rejection.
