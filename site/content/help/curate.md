## Bring Tracks into the Library {#importing}

Open **Settings**, relaunch the **Tracks directory** Setup guide, and choose your music folder. Click **Save & scan folder** to import audio from that folder and its subfolders. Tracks stay where they are; keep the drive connected when you play them. Already-imported paths are skipped.

In the Desktop shell, you can also drag audio files or folders onto the Library. Drop them onto a Playlist to import and add them there together. File-drop import needs the Desktop shell; in a browser, use the folder-path field in the Setup guide.

Open **Unprocessed** in the sidebar to work through new Tracks. Select a Track, listen, and correct its title and artist. Check BPM and Key against what you hear; use [Analysis](../analysis/index.html) when the grid needs attention.

## Assign Tags and Energy {#tags}

Click a Tag in the selected Track's tag panel to add or remove it. Assignments save immediately. In Library keyboard focus, press **T**, type part of a Tag name, choose a result with the arrow keys, and press **Enter**. Press **Escape** to leave the search.

Tags belong to Tag Categories. Use the tag-management control to edit that structure. Genre is a Tag Category; **Energy** is a separate 1–5 attribute. Press **E**, then **1–5**, to set Energy in Library keyboard focus.

Use the filter bar to combine Tags, text, Energy, Key, and BPM while browsing.

## Arrange a Playlist {#playlists}

1. Click **+ New… → Playlist** in the sidebar and enter a name.
2. Drag Tracks onto it, or right-click selected Tracks and choose **Add to playlist**.
3. Open the Playlist and sort by **# / Play order** before dragging rows into position.

Sorting by BPM or another column changes the view, not Play order. Clear filters if positional dragging is unavailable. Each Track appears only once in a Playlist. For saved handovers between Tracks, [plan a Set](../sets/index.html#planning).

## Archive and bring back Tracks {#archive}

Right-click a Track and choose **Archive track**. It leaves active browsing, discovery, and Export; its audio and metadata remain.

Archiving also removes Playlist memberships. Open **Archived**, right-click the Track, and choose **Unarchive** to return it to the active Library. Previous Playlist memberships are not restored: add it back where needed. Scanning the same folder again does not undo an Archive verdict.
