## Start with the keyboard {#keyboard}

Open **PERFORM**. The left-hand keys control A or C; the right-hand keys control B or D. **[** switches left focus, **]** switches right focus. Check which Deck is focused before playing.

- **D / K**: play or pause left / right.
- **F / J**: hold Cue.
- **Z X C V / M , . /**: Hot Cues 1–4; **Shift** plus a pad clears it.
- **B / N**: toggle the loop.
- **A / S** and **L / ;**: jump backward / forward.

Press **?** or **F1** for the keyboard map. Typing and open dialogs suppress Deck shortcuts. **=** toggles Quantize.

The number row controls Beat FX: **1–5** select A/B/C/D/MST, **6/7** halve/double length, **8/9** change effect, **0** plus mouse adjusts depth, and **-** toggles FX. See [Beat FX](../beat-fx/index.html) for effect controls.

## Load the next Track {#loading}

Drag a Library Track onto the intended Deck, or use its right-click **Load** action. Wait for loading to finish before judging the waveform or transport response.

For keyboard browsing, press **Tab**, move through Tracks with the arrows, and press **A**, **B**, **C**, or **D** to load that Deck; C/D require four-Deck mode. **Escape** returns keyboard focus to the Decks. [Follow](../follow/index.html#suggestions) can keep this list matched to what is playing.

## Shape the blend {#mixer}

Drag the on-screen channel faders, EQ, filter, and crossfader, or hold a control key while moving the mouse:

- **Q W E R**: left filter, high, mid, low.
- **P O I U**: right filter, high, mid, low.
- **G / H**: left / right channel fader.

Release the key to finish the gesture. If a playing Track is silent, check its channel level, crossfader assignment, and kills, then [audio routing](../audio/index.html). The clip above shows on-screen mixer drags.

## Nudge with mouse jog {#mouse-jog}

Hold **T / Y** and move horizontally to jog the focused left / right Deck. With Vinyl on, **Shift+T / Shift+Y** arms scratching; playback continues until you move. Adjust sensitivity and smoothing under **Settings → Keyboard + mouse → Mouse jog**.

## Separate and control stems {#stems}

Hover the stem row beneath the pads and click **EXTRACT STEMS**. When processing finishes, choose **STEMS READY — RELOAD** to load the separated audio onto that Deck.

Click vocals, drums, bass, or other to kill or restore that part; **Shift-click** solos it. Disabled stem buttons mean stems are not loaded yet, even if extraction has just finished.
