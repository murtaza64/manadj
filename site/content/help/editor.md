## Edit {#editing}

1. Open **EDIT**; choose a mix in the right-hand picker.
2. **Select (V)** selects and moves slots; the outgoing slot anchors a Transition. **Pan (H)** moves the view; **Jump (J)** edits jumps/pauses.
3. Open a control lane's breakpoint editor. Click to add nodes, drag to adjust, double-click to delete. **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** undoes; **Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z** redoes. Saved mixes autosave.

## Audition {#audition}

4. Press play or **Space**. Audition uses the shared Decks and Mixer; opening a mix alone does not interrupt playback. Wait while loading, or press again to cancel.
5. Pause with editor transport. Moving live Deck/Mixer controls takes over playback. Auditions create no Takes.

## Promote {#promote}

6. An unpromoted Take opens in **REVIEW**. Edit, audition, then **Promote** to save a Transition or Routine. Review edits do not persist before promotion; original evidence remains.

## Blank mix {#blank-mix}

7. Choose **+ New blank mix**; drag Library Tracks onto the canvas. Two slots save as a **Transition**, three or more as a **Routine**; fewer than two remain unsaved. Adding or removing a slot across that boundary converts the saved mix; Set pins follow.
