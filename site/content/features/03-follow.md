Turn on **Follow** for a Deck to see next-Track suggestions as you
play. The list follows what's loaded, so you can keep browsing while
the mix changes.

Your choices come first: **Known** pairs connected by a saved
Transition, Cameo, or a **Linked** association. Repeated live pairs
come next as **Observed**. Then **Compatible** suggestions draw on
Key, Tags, shared artists, BPM, and Energy. A clashing Key alone
doesn't rule a Track out. For more variety, **Temperature** reshuffles
the Compatible suggestions without moving them above your curated pairs.
