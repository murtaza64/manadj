Refresh your SoundCloud likes to see what you already own and choose
what to download. Mixes and clips are filtered out by default, and
downloaded titles and artist names are cleaned up.

If SoundCloud can't supply the audio, search **Soulseek** and choose a
file. manadj records where its downloads came from, ready for you to
curate alongside the rest of your Library.
