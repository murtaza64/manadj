Use your keyboard and mouse to mix on four Decks. Keys choose the
control; mouse movement rides it. Your Library stays below the
waveforms, ready for the next Track.

- Press <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> to play the focused left or right Deck.
- Hold <kbd>Q</kbd> or <kbd>P</kbd> and move the mouse to sweep its filter.
- Hold <kbd>G</kbd> or <kbd>H</kbd> and move the mouse to ride channel volume;
  double-tap to cut or return to full volume.

**KBD** labels show keys on the controls. <kbd>F1</kbd> opens
help. Key Lock is on by default.

Prefer to grab a Track? Drag it from the Library onto a Deck to load it.
For a different blend, split a Track into **stems** and mute or solo
its vocals, drums, bass, or other instruments while you play.
