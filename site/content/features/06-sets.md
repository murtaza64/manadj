Arrange Tracks in a **Set** and choose how each hands over: a saved
Transition, a Take you've selected, or a hard cut. Leave a handover
**Unresolved** to use the pair's best saved Transition, falling back
to a cut if there isn't one.

Press **Play set** and the **Conductor** executes those handovers on
the Decks and Mixer. Touch a Deck or Mixer control to take over;
the music keeps playing. **Pick up** resumes the plan when your live
Deck state lines up with it.
