New Tracks are analyzed for **Beatgrid**, Key, waveform, and
**Structure**. If beat detection fails, the Track is flagged for
attention. Energy estimates appear where you haven't set a rating.

When you're ready, stamp Hot Cues 1–4 as a ladder into the detected
drop. Only empty slots are filled; analysis doesn't write cues on its
own. Adjust the drop cue yourself when your ears disagree.
