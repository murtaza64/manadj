While you mix live, manadj logs Deck and Mixer events in a **Session**,
not an audio recording. Detected handovers become **Takes**; a tease
that returns to the original Track can become a **Cameo** Take.

Find a moment on the timeline and replay it through the Decks. Grab a
fader to take over live, or cut out a missed blend by hand for review.
Compare a few attempts, then Promote the ones you want to use again.
