Open a Take in the **Mix editor** as an editable draft. Captured fades
become envelopes you can reshape; jumps stay editable too. Change
where a Track enters, shorten a fade, or adjust a cut, then audition
through the same Decks and Mixer you use live.

**Promote** saves the draft as a **Transition** you can use again. The
original Take stays intact. The same timeline handles **Cameos** and
multi-Track **Routines**, and you can start from a blank mix when you
already know what you want to try.
