Prefer physical controls? A supported **Controller** drives the same
Decks and Mixer, with pad and transport lights following the app.

Mappings are available for Pioneer **DDJ-GRV6**, Pioneer **DDJ-SB3**,
and Hercules **DJControl Inpulse 300 MK2**. The GRV6 controls all four
Decks and Beat FX; the SB3 uses a layer switch to reach Decks C and D.
