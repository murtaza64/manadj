Falsifiable question: does a MECHANICAL metaphor (gears, springs,
clutches) make bpm/phrase structure legible in a way particle systems
haven't?
Concept: a mechanical solar system where the 24 bands group into nested
orbital mechanisms. Lows drive solid eccentric hubs and flywheels, mids
articulated arms, highs fine gear teeth and sparks. BPM fixes orbital
periods to rational bar lengths; phrase position visibly winds springs;
section boundaries release and rebuild the mechanism into a new
symmetry. Kick = a clutch engagement that physically advances the whole
gear train; snare = brief mid/high-gated metal GLINTS at gear-mesh
points (light catching teeth — not filings/dust). Buildup winds springs
tighter, saturated edge-color travel accelerates, orbital spacing
narrows; drop releases stored angle into a decisive topology change +
sustained fast operation.
Invariants: period-locked to bpm (rational ratios); phrase = visible
spring tension; kick advances physical state (nothing merely flashes).
Degrees of freedom: mechanism vocabulary, symmetry families, rendering
(neon SDF linework recommended).
Layout: OFF-CENTER — place the primary hub at a rule-of-thirds point,
mechanisms extend asymmetrically across the frame; use occlusion,
silhouette, parallax. Never a centered mandala.
Assigned tech: beat phase + bpm (primary), bar/phrase/section via
`beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`, 24-band spectrum for mechanism
activity, impulses.
Anti-resemblance: not Orbit / Charge Bloom (no free-floating particle
orbits — everything is CONNECTED machinery); no dust; white-hot contact
sparks restricted to small gear-mesh regions.
