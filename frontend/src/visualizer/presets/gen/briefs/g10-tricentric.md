# g10-tricentric

Candidate: g10-tricentric   Kind: novel (flat wave; Vissonance
'Tricentric' tradition)
Human ask: flat color schemes, less noisy.
Concept: concentric flat polygon rings (triangles/hexagons) receding
toward an off-center vanishing point — a clean geometric tunnel of
OUTLINED and SOLID flat rings, no glow. Ring scale/advance speed rides
bandsSlow.low (motion law); ring stroke weight = mids; every Nth ring
solid-filled from a flat 4-color scheme, others thin outlines on the
flat background. Kick = the nearest ring SNAPS one step closer +
solid-fills for a beat (pop, not flash); snare = one ring rotates 30
degrees (hard step); bar = polygon sides cycle (3->4->6, quantized);
phrase = scheme rotates one color; section = scheme swap + vanishing
point relocates (hard cut); drop = rings alternate solid/outline in a
strobe-SAFE pattern (chroma alternation at <=2Hz, never luminance
strobing) + tunnel speed rides max(drop, energy) via bandsSlow.
FLAT LAW (this wave): solid matte fills, hard edges, a committed palette of 3-5 flat colors, NO glow/bloom/additive haze/feedback smear/noise textures/particles/swirling fluids. Motion is TRANSFORMS (translate/rotate/scale/flip/wipe) and COLOR SWAPS, cleanly eased or hard-cut on the grid. Depth via flat-shaded polygons is allowed (Vissonance tradition: Iris/Barred/HillFog/Tricentric — three.js flat-shaded FFT geometry). Dark flat background, not black-void-with-glow. Effects are welcome (cuts, pops, flips, wipes) — the target is LESS NOISY than our glowy/spacey winners, not static.
Standing law: docs/visualizer-ga.md — taste calibration, hard safety, feedback contraction (mostly moot here: prefer NO feedback buffer), MOTION SMOOTHNESS (velocity terms ride frame.bandsSlow ?? frame.bands; instantaneous bands/impulse only for pops/punches). Phrase/section via `beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`.
