calligraphic strokes across the frame. Left/right are spatial
trajectories, not two scopes: correlation braids them together,
divergence opens negative space, phase disagreement creates loops. Low
energy sets stroke WEIGHT plus a solid paper-deformation pulse on kicks;
mids set curvature; highs add edge fibrils and brief ink FLICKS (gated
away from kicks — not spray/dust). Phrase evolution builds a
composition: establish a radical, elaborate, compress, then one heavy
decisive DROP STROKE; ink keeps flowing on sustained energy. Buildups
increase pen velocity and saturated ink travel on a calm luminance
floor. Section boundaries wash or fold the page into a new writing
direction.
Invariants: strokes are continuous and composed (persistent canvas via
feedback, chroma-preserving soft knee); diagonal/lateral composition;
never an oscilloscope readout look (no axes, no centered horizontal
trace).

trail), page-wash choreography, ink palette behavior.
