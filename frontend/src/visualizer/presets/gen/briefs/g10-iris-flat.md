# g10-iris-flat

Candidate: g10-iris-flat   Kind: novel (flat wave; Vissonance 'Iris'
tradition)
Human ask: flat color schemes, less noisy than our glowy/spacey strong
presets.
Concept: a radial iris of flat wedge petals (24 — one per spectrum
band, frequency-ordered around the circle). Each petal is a SOLID
matte wedge whose LENGTH is its band level (bandsSlow for the resting
motion; instantaneous only for the kick pop) and whose color comes from
a flat 4-color scheme (e.g. cream/coral/teal/ink — commit to schemes,
swap the whole scheme per section, hard cut). Petal edges crisp. Kick =
the iris APERTURE pops open one ring step (transform, no flash) and
snaps back; snare = alternating petals flip to the scheme's accent
color for one beat; phrase = petal count/grouping re-arranges (hard
cut); drop = petals extend full-radius and the scheme INVERTS
(background <-> petal colors) for the plateau, riding max(drop,
energy). Buildup = iris slowly contracts (tension by geometry).
FLAT LAW (this wave): solid matte fills, hard edges, a committed palette of 3-5 flat colors, NO glow/bloom/additive haze/feedback smear/noise textures/particles/swirling fluids. Motion is TRANSFORMS (translate/rotate/scale/flip/wipe) and COLOR SWAPS, cleanly eased or hard-cut on the grid. Depth via flat-shaded polygons is allowed (Vissonance tradition: Iris/Barred/HillFog/Tricentric — three.js flat-shaded FFT geometry). Dark flat background, not black-void-with-glow. Effects are welcome (cuts, pops, flips, wipes) — the target is LESS NOISY than our glowy/spacey winners, not static.
Standing law: docs/visualizer-ga.md — taste calibration, hard safety, feedback contraction (mostly moot here: prefer NO feedback buffer), MOTION SMOOTHNESS (velocity terms ride frame.bandsSlow ?? frame.bands; instantaneous bands/impulse only for pops/punches). Phrase/section via `beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`.
