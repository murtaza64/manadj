Falsifiable question: does building architecture from DARKNESS (cut out
of a saturated color volume) fix the legibility/smoke failures that
killed the bright-column attempts?
Concept: spectrum bands cut windows and flying buttresses out of a
saturated volumetric color field — the structure is the ABSENCE of
light. Lows determine massive bays, mids arches, highs fine tracery.

lattice/monolith nausea note applies). Kick = solid foundation
compression: floor and vault flex together — no smoke, no kick
particles. Snare catches brief glints only in localized window light.
Buildup raises colored light behind the windows and closes the nave
toward a vanishing point; drop opens the roof and expands into a
persistent wide hall on max(drop, energy). Section boundaries change
plan topology: basilica, radial vault, bridge, impossible stair.
Invariants: strict contrast hierarchy (dark structure, luminous volume);
slow occlusion changes, never full-screen flashes; camera velocity
capped.
Degrees of freedom: architectural plan vocabulary, volume shading
(Shadertoy-style FBM emission volume recommended), window tracery
detail.
Layout: one-point perspective with the vanishing point OFF-CENTER;
asymmetric nave.
Assigned tech: 24-band spectrum (primary), bpm/beat phase, phrase/
section via `beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`, trend.
Anti-resemblance: no bright columns (that's the dead cathedral), no
smoke/dust, no tunnel feedback warp.
