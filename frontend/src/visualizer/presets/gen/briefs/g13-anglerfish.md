Territory: living things & narrative. Concept: an ABYSS predation scene.
A single anglerfish silhouette hangs at frame center in a black deep-sea
column. Its bioluminescent LURE is the bass pulse. A SCHOOL of small prey
fish (spectrum-band shoal) drifts in the water column, drawn toward the
lure; on a strong beat the anglerfish STRIKES and the nearest prey vanish
(eaten) in a puff. Legible predator/prey causality: you can see WHY every
fish moved (toward the light) and WHY it died (the strike).

Falsifiable question: can a staged predator/prey scene with legible actors
(lure = bass, shoal = spectrum, strike = beat) read as NARRATIVE rather
than a particle wash — the thing g04-story died for (its LOOK, not its
premise)?

Anti-resemblance: nursery/flock/crowd/breath/frost families exist; this is
NOT a swarm of identical dots — there is ONE predator, a legible lure, and
prey with a goal (phototaxis). No dust/powder medium. Deep-sea creature
premise is explicitly invited by the dossier.

Band vocabulary (distinct per band):
- LOW (bass) — the LURE. The anglerfish's esca (lure bulb) brightness +
  radius rides bands.low; impulse.low = a hard PULSE of the lure (a beacon
  throb that lights the water near it). Heavy bass = big bright lure.
- MID — the anglerfish BODY presence: its silhouette looms larger / opens
  its jaw wider with bandsSlow.mid (a slow attribute — rides slow bands).
  Mid impulse widens the gape.
- HIGH — the prey SHOAL shimmer: each prey fish flashes a thin scale-glint
  (discrete hairline, not glow) on impulse.high; high band sets shoal
  liveliness (how fast they dart). Distinct hi-freq filaments, not dust.
- centroid/flatness — water TINT + prey palette: tonal (low flatness) =
  cohesive jewel shoal; noisy = scattered, desaturated murk. Centroid
  swings the abyss tint cold→warm.

Dramatic kicks / quantized grammar:
- STRIKE on the beat grid: gated by impulse.low AND a drop/energy drive —
  the anglerfish lunges toward the densest prey cluster, prey within the
  strike cone are EATEN (removed with a brief ink puff, source-over, no
  full-field flash). One strike per beat max (quantized, legible), <=2/s.
- DROP = feeding FRENZY: strikes ride max(drop, energy); the lure blazes,
  the shoal is pulled hard toward it, multiple prey consumed. Sustained,
  not a transition twitch.
- BUILDUP = the shoal GATHERS: prey converge and circle the lure (tense,
  alive — bait ball tightening), dread before the strike. No stacked
  suppressors, never eerily still.
- SECTION boundary (ladder %16) = the current shoal disperses and a FRESH
  shoal of a new palette swims in (hard cut); anglerfish re-poses. Phrase
  (%4) = lure color shift.
