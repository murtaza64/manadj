Territory: ABSTRACT MATHEMATICS & GEOMETRY not yet tried. A TRUCHET tile
grid: each cell holds one of two rotations of a quarter-arc (or diagonal)
tile, and the arcs join across cell edges into flowing maze/weave contours.
The whole picture is a lattice of hard-edged tiles that FLIP orientation on
quantized metric events, re-routing the contour network each beat. This is
the archetypal "quantized grammar" geometry — discrete state per cell,
integer flips, legible causality (you can see WHICH tiles just turned).

Anti-resemblance clearance: not a hypno spiral, not stained glass (Voronoi
cells), not mirror strata, not flat Vissonance polygon families (iris/
barred/hillfog/tricentric are concentric/columnar, not a tiling grid), not
truchet-adjacent existing families (none exist). This is a tiling automaton.

Falsifiable question: does a Truchet grid whose cells flip on the metric
grid — arcs re-joining into new contours per beat, EQ-killable per band —
read as legible flip-driven mathematics rather than noise?

Invariants:
- FLAT: two committed contrasting colors paint the arcs and a dark tile
  floor; hard edges, no glow, no blur, no feedback buffer. Canvas 2D.
- Flips are DISCRETE and quantized to the metric grid — never a smooth
  interpolation of a rotating tile. A flipped cell snaps to its new
  orientation; a brief highlight marks it (legible causality).
- Motion (any grid drift/breathing, arc thickness velocity) rides bandsSlow;
  instantaneous bands/impulse drive flip counts and highlight pops only.
- Legible per-band vocabulary:
    LOW  = kicks flip a BLOCK of cells at once (a square patch snaps to new
           orientations) — the structural, solid response (gated impulse.low
           so not kick-powder) + arc STROKE WEIGHT rides low.
    MID  = snares flip a full ROW or COLUMN (a hard sweep across the grid).
    HIGH = hats flip scattered SINGLE cells (sparse individual re-routes) +
           set a thin secondary "seam" arc color on flipped cells.
- Distinct-per-band EQ kills: killing LOW stops block flips + thins arcs;
  killing MID stops row/column sweeps; killing HIGH stops single-cell
  sparkle. Each removal visibly takes something away.
- Photosensitivity: highlights are localized cells; no fullscreen flash.
