# g10-poster

Candidate: g10-poster   Kind: novel (flat wave; Swiss/Bauhaus poster +
Memphis motion graphics)
Human ask: flat color schemes, less noisy; effects welcome.
Concept: a bold flat POSTER that dances: 3-6 large solid geometric
actors (circle, bar, triangle, zigzag, arc) on a flat field, arranged
by a trackId-genome layout. Lows = the big circle's scale breathing
(bandsSlow); mids = bar/zigzag skew and travel; highs = small accent
dots' count (discrete, not particles — max 12, grid-placed). Kick =
one actor does a hard transform POP (scale 1.15x snap + settle,
rotating which actor per beat); snare = two actors SWAP colors
(instant); bar = layout micro-shift (quantized); phrase = full layout
recomposition (hard cut, genome-sequenced); section = palette swap
across committed flat schemes (mustard/brick/cream/navy;
pink/red/white/black; teal/orange/sand/charcoal...). Drop = the poster
goes MAXIMAL: actors enlarge, background flips to the loudest color,
composition pulses in beat-locked steps riding max(drop, energy).
Buildup = actors crowd toward center (tension).
FLAT LAW (this wave): solid matte fills, hard edges, a committed palette of 3-5 flat colors, NO glow/bloom/additive haze/feedback smear/noise textures/particles/swirling fluids. Motion is TRANSFORMS (translate/rotate/scale/flip/wipe) and COLOR SWAPS, cleanly eased or hard-cut on the grid. Depth via flat-shaded polygons is allowed (Vissonance tradition: Iris/Barred/HillFog/Tricentric — three.js flat-shaded FFT geometry). Dark flat background, not black-void-with-glow. Effects are welcome (cuts, pops, flips, wipes) — the target is LESS NOISY than our glowy/spacey winners, not static.
Standing law: docs/visualizer-ga.md — taste calibration, hard safety, feedback contraction (mostly moot here: prefer NO feedback buffer), MOTION SMOOTHNESS (velocity terms ride frame.bandsSlow ?? frame.bands; instantaneous bands/impulse only for pops/punches). Phrase/section via `beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`.
