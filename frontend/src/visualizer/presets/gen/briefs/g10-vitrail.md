# g10-vitrail

Candidate: g10-vitrail   Kind: novel (flat wave x TONALITY — chameleon
idea #3, clean-room: a completely new visual metaphor, do NOT reuse
g08-chameleon's aurora/warp language)
Human ask: another pass at the tonality idea (colorful when melodic,
kinetic-not-colorful when percussive), in a flat visual language.
Concept: a STAINED-GLASS WINDOW (flat!): a voronoi/grid lead-line
lattice of solid color panes on a dark matte field. TONALITY AXIS
(derive as chameleon does: ~750ms EMA of (1 - flatness), reduced by a
~1s impulse-density window):
- TONAL pole: the panes GLOW WITH COLOR variety — each pane holds a
  solid saturated color from a wide flat gamut (>= 6 hue families
  visible; spread widens the gamut), and panes RE-COLOR in slow
  harmonic waves (color does the work; geometry nearly still). Kick =
  a colored pane-lighting wave crosses the window (solid color steps,
  no bloom).
- PERCUSSIVE pole: panes drain to 2-3 desaturated stony tones and the
  LATTICE goes kinetic: lead-lines jolt and re-fracture on kicks (the
  voronoi re-seeds locally — glass visibly re-breaks), snare = one
  pane SHATTERS into sub-panes (hard-edged, flat, no particles),
  buildup = fracture density rises, drop = the whole window re-breaks
  in beat-locked steps riding max(drop, energy). Motion/fracture
  carries the energy, color does not.
- TRANSITION: a traveling recoloring front (flat color floods pane by
  pane), per the chameleon front idea but in discrete pane steps.
Section = new lattice topology + new gamut. Legible, hard-edged, zero
glow.
FLAT LAW (this wave): solid matte fills, hard edges, a committed palette of 3-5 flat colors, NO glow/bloom/additive haze/feedback smear/noise textures/particles/swirling fluids. Motion is TRANSFORMS (translate/rotate/scale/flip/wipe) and COLOR SWAPS, cleanly eased or hard-cut on the grid. Depth via flat-shaded polygons is allowed (Vissonance tradition: Iris/Barred/HillFog/Tricentric — three.js flat-shaded FFT geometry). Dark flat background, not black-void-with-glow. Effects are welcome (cuts, pops, flips, wipes) — the target is LESS NOISY than our glowy/spacey winners, not static.
Standing law: docs/visualizer-ga.md — taste calibration, hard safety, feedback contraction (mostly moot here: prefer NO feedback buffer), MOTION SMOOTHNESS (velocity terms ride frame.bandsSlow ?? frame.bands; instantaneous bands/impulse only for pops/punches). Phrase/section via `beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`.
