Falsifiable question: can a LATERAL two-deck stage (plates meeting at

Concept: two deck-owned continental plates meet at a vertical seam
positioned by the crossfader. Each audible deck contributes a saturated
traveling color field and a spectrum-shaped coastline: low band raises
solid landmass, mids buckle ridges, highs etch fine faults. Fader
movement advances one plate over the other; EQ kills erase the
corresponding scale of terrain; doubles interlock strata. Kicks drive
localized compression waves through the audible plate; snares crack
GLOWING FISSURES along existing faults (mid/high-gated light in the
cracks — NOT dust; dust fatigue rule in the kit applies). Buildup: fault closes, vivid pressure veins
rise (never dimmer). Drop: rupture — plates separate, sustained motion
on max(drop, energy). Section boundaries rotate the collision axis or
switch regime (subduction/rift/transform).
Invariants: seam position ← crossfader; band identity carried by
terrain SCALE (low=mass, mid=ridge, high=fault); rupture is
displacement + color travel + localized edge light, never a full-field
flash.
Degrees of freedom: terrain rendering (heightfield shading vs contour
lines), regime vocabulary, plate texture.
Assigned tech: deck state (levels/EQ/fader/doubles) primary; impulses;
trend; section tiers via `beat.ladderBarIndex ?? beat.barIndex`.
Anti-resemblance: NO centered radial glow, no feedback-warp tunnel skin,
no cosine-palette default look. This must not read as a Tunnel variant.
