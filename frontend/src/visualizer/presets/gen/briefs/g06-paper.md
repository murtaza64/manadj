Concept: layered paper silhouettes, apertures, shadows, and scene
changes. A stable protagonist shape carries low-band mass; mids
articulate joints and scenery; highs cut PERFORATIONS — pinpricks of
backlight through the paper (not confetti/dust). Each four-bar phrase
performs one readable action in a 16-bar chapter arc: reveal, pursuit,
compression, release. Song genome (trackId hash) chooses silhouette
families and staging direction. Color travels as saturated LIGHT BEHIND
the paper; the actors stay dark cutouts. Kick = solid stage impact or
aperture punch. Buildup rapidly shifts backlight hue through the cutouts
while scenery keeps moving; drop tears open the central aperture into a
broad animated world on max(drop, energy). Section changes are a
continuous curtain wipe, never a flash.
Invariants: silhouette legibility (strict contrast hierarchy: dark paper
vs luminous backlight); one readable action per phrase; staging is
LATERAL (stage left/right depth layers), not radial.
Degrees of freedom: silhouette families, scenery vocabulary, layer
count/parallax.
Assigned tech: phrase/section tiers via `beat.ladderBarIndex ??
beat.barIndex` (primary), trackId genome, impulses, trend.
Anti-resemblance: no ring/orbit stage, no glow-core, no particles; this
must read as paper and light.
