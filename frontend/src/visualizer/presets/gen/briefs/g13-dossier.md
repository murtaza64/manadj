# Gen-13 creative dossier (overnight freestyle)

Human mandate: "get creative... keys: variety, responsiveness, kinetic,
color, effects, creativity." Each agent invents its own presets within an
assigned territory, writes the brief file itself (gNN-slug.md, this
format), then the candidate.

## What WINS with this human (evidence, 13 generations)
- STRUCTURE and MATERIAL identity (materia line, tunnel line, solar-crown)
- QUANTIZED grammar: hard cuts on the metric grid, integer jumps,
  quantized->fluid contrast on drops (voyage-hardcut won gen-7)
- LEGIBLE CAUSALITY: you can see WHY something moved (mirror-ladder
  storytelling, crowd/piston/shadow briefs); never a blank or washy screen
- EARNED BRIGHTNESS: dark floors, light with a cause (prime-embers)
- Dramatic kicks (solid, scene-scale), strong drops riding max(drop,
  energy), vibrant buildups (tense-but-alive)
- Distinct per-band vocabularies; EQ kills that visibly remove something

## What DIES
- Washy translucent fields, instrument looks (scopes), static scenes,
  too-fast-to-read motion, erratic per-frame jitter, single-color frames,
  glow soup, blank screens, smooth interpolation of discrete things
